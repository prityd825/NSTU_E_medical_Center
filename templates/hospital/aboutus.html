About us

The Medical Center of NSTU

Located on the ground floor of the Administration Building (Room 117), the medical center offers free general practitioner and emergency care to everyone in the university community: students, teachers, officers, staff, and the families of teachers, officers and staff.

Our staff are here to help with your problems. We treat you as a responsible adult and offer careful support without judgement.

Our services

Routine check-ups

Pulse, temperature, blood pressure and general health checks.

Illness & injury

Treatment of illness, injury and other physical problems.

Minor procedures

Minor surgery and wound care, such as draining an abscess or boil.

Sports injuries

On-field management of minor sports injuries.

Asthma emergencies

Nebulization and oxygen for asthmatic patients.

Blood sugar test

Free blood sugar checks with a glucometer.

Travel medicine

Travel health advice for departments.

Specialist referral

Referral to a specialist when your condition needs it.

Medical certificates

Medical certificates and pre-employment fitness checks.
